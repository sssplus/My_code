How to use PodcastForge efficiently

From a podcast link to a week of content in five steps. Each tool hands its output to the next.

1

Add an API key (bring your own)

Open the workspace and paste any supported key in the key bar — Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, or NVIDIA. PodcastForge auto-detects the provider. Tip: a free Groq or Gemini key also unlocks audio transcription, so you never need a paid OpenAI key just to transcribe.

2

Find a podcast & pull its transcript

Use Discover to search Apple Podcasts, or paste an Apple/Spotify/YouTube link, an RSS URL, or a show name into Transcripts. Episodes that publish a transcript are pulled instantly; for the rest, click Generate from audio to transcribe with your OpenAI, Groq, or Gemini key.

3

Generate repurposed content

Send a transcript to the Generator and pick formats — blog post, X/Twitter thread, LinkedIn post, show notes, newsletter, and more. Run the Agent to auto-repurpose into several formats at once.

4

Go deeper with the specialist tools

Content Miner extracts quotes, hooks, and insights section by section. Script Studio drafts full episode scripts. Music Brief suggests intro/outro direction. AI Stack Tracker audits the tools mentioned in an episode.

5

Track usage & manage keys

Click your account chip (top-right) for today's usage against your plan, recent activity, and connected keys — including which of them can transcribe audio. The free plan has per-tool daily caps; the trial and paid plans are unlimited.

Pro tips for speed

  • ⌨️ Press Enter in the Discover and Transcripts inputs to search/load without reaching for the mouse.
  • 🔁 Tools are chained — the → Generator, → Agent, and → Miner buttons on a pulled transcript skip copy-paste entirely.
  • 🎙️ No published transcript? Pick a shorter episode (audio is capped at 25 MB) and use Generate from audio.
  • 💸 Mix providers: a fast free model (Groq/Gemini) for drafts, a stronger model (Claude/GPT-4o) for final polish.
  • 📊 Watch the daily caps in your account panel so you don't get interrupted mid-workflow on the free plan.

Frequently asked questions

What PodcastForge is, how your keys are handled, and what each plan includes.

What is PodcastForge?
PodcastForge turns any podcast episode into ready-to-publish content. Pull (or generate) a transcript, then repurpose it into blog posts, social threads, newsletters, show notes, episode scripts, and more — using your own AI provider key.
Do I need to pay for an AI subscription?
You bring your own API key, so you pay your provider directly for what you use. Several providers have generous free tiers — a free Groq or Google Gemini key is enough to both generate content and transcribe audio. PodcastForge's own Free plan adds daily caps; the trial and paid plans lift them.
Which providers are supported?
Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, and NVIDIA NIM for content generation. For turning audio into transcripts, use an OpenAI, Groq, or Gemini key (Whisper / multimodal). The app auto-detects the provider from the key format.
How are my API keys stored and used?
If you're signed in, keys are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on the server and used server-side to call providers — they're never returned to the browser. Without an account, your key stays in your browser and calls the provider directly. Either way you can remove a key anytime from your account panel.
Can I transcribe an episode that has no transcript?
Yes. In the Transcripts tool, episodes without a published transcript show a Generate from audio button that transcribes the audio with your OpenAI, Groq, or Gemini key. Audio is capped at 25 MB per episode, so shorter episodes transcribe fastest. Your account panel shows which of your keys can transcribe.
How does the free trial and pricing work?
New accounts get a 15-day full trial with unlimited access. After that you can stay on the Free plan (per-tool daily caps), or choose Fixed Pro (unlimited, flat monthly) or Pay-as-you-go. You can switch plans anytime from the pricing section or paywall.
Is explicit or adult content allowed?
No. Podcasts flagged explicit/adult (via the iTunes/RSS flags) are filtered out of search and can't be pulled, and transcripts are screened so explicit material is blocked.
Can I sign in with Google or GitHub?
Yes, when the operator has configured social login. Otherwise, email + password works out of the box. Either way your verified email identifies your account.
